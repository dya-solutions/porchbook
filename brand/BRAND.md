# Porchbook brand

Agreed with JT on 20 November 2026. Porchbook's brand is **deep olive** and the **circle-of-people logo** (five people, arms linked, nobody at the head: fellowship). This replaces the six full church themes.

## The rule: olive frame, church accent

- **Porchbook's olive is always the frame**: the logo, top bar, bottom bar, main buttons, the selected tab, links, switches and focus rings. Every porch looks like Porchbook.
- **Each church picks one accent colour** (below) for its own touches only: its name tag and badges, its cover strip, its pinned announcements, its office icons and its room chips. The accent never replaces olive on buttons or bars.
- **Each church still shows its own identity** through its logo (or initials), its name and, later, its cover photo.
- **Members choose Light or Dark** for themselves. Light is the default. Dawn / Dusk / Night stays as a gentle dimmer.

The SVG logos use `currentColor`, so they take the colour of the text around them: **white on olive**, **olive on cream or white**, **night olive on dark**. Never put the logo in any other colour, stretch it, or put it on a busy photo.

Below 32 px tall, use the mark alone (no word).

## Type

- **Fredoka** (Google Fonts, 500 weight) for titles and names: it is the closest free match to the logo's rounded lettering. If the logo's real font becomes known, use that instead.
- **Source Sans 3** for body text, as now.

## Reactions and alerts

Love, notification badges and "Live now" use the porch's accent colour, so the brand olive stays calm and the accent gives the spark.
