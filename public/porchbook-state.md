## What it is

Porchbook is a private porch for Phaneroo Ministries Mbarara. Orange is #f8a018. Green is #90c840. Paper and cards are black. Titles are Fraunces. Body text is Source Sans 3. The ground is the porch photograph. There is no startup video and no black opening screen. The app opens straight onto the porch.

## Light

Dawn is the normal porch. Dusk is a warm dim. Night, stored as lamp, blacks the porch to about 96 percent, except on the tour, where Night only dims to about half so the photograph still shows. Do not add another lighting system.

## Tour

The tour is /next. Pray and My 20 are the wide doors. Under them, three by two: Events, Serve, Transport, Groups, Offices, Feedback. Feedback is a warm dark door, not a black hole. Door colors are orange, green, gold, and that quiet brown. Dawn, Dusk, and Night sit under the doors. Under Night, in one quiet row: Set prayer music, and Get the Android app. The Android link hides inside the installed app.

## Home

The bottom bar is Feed, Chat, Invite, and Refresh. Tour and Read are in the header switch, not the bar. The desktop side holds the rooms Inspire, Inform, and Inquire, plus Rhemaclip.

Posts are glass. Each card is 86 percent of the column, with a wide gap between cards. The glass is a pale pane across the top, clearer below, a thin bright rim, and a small light on the top edge. The porch shows through. Post writing is white.

The avatar sits on the left. Pin, Edit, and Remove, or call and message, sit on the right. The full name, the room, the team, and the time are on their own lines under that row. Tapping the avatar or the name opens the person.

Love, Like, Touching, Deep, Reply, and Lock sit on small dark pills, about 55 percent black, only as wide as the words. The green letters stay green.

There is no cartoon. The still preacher photograph is the empty-feed portrait. Photos in posts cannot be saved or long-pressed. Screenshots of people's images are not accepted. Uploaded photos are compressed. A matching photo is refused.

## Posting panel

The composer is open at the top of the feed. It is clear green glass with a deeper leaf-green rim. The writing box is dark, about half black, so the words can be read. The hint is green. Typed words are white. That box has no dark letter-edge.

Inspire, Inform, and Inquire have icons. The chosen one is a solid leaf-green pill with dark letters. Rhemaclip is green text with its lamp. Photo is a green circle. Mine and Post are solid leaf-green buttons with dark letters. Those solid buttons also have no letter-edge.

## Letters

Every other letter in the app has a thin dark edge on all four sides, so green, orange, and white can be read on the photograph. There is no black cushion behind the name or the room line.

## Sounds

Six upload slots. Five are fixed: tap, sign-out, open, office bell, post-bend. The sixth can be assigned to one of those, including the water-drop used when someone reacts. A short press on a post edge plays only as long as the finger stays. A long press plays the whole bend sound. Holding the middle of a post scales it to 0.96 with that same sound. The bend sound has no hiss.

## Read, offices, transport, calls

Read is the shelf. Books share one size that fits their titles. The Bible and the notebook are realistic sizes. Bible texture is the leather cover that was supplied. A book can be selected and deleted from the shelf and from the Read window. The notebook that sat between the Bible and the Devotional stays removed. Office files stay in offices. Everyone else uploads photos only.

Transport is a ride to a church activity and back. Someone who already has a way home can say so. People who do not give a number get the details in their Porchbook inbox. The door says Transport, not Free transport.
